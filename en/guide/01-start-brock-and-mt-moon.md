## Step-by-step walkthrough

**1.** Check the PC in your room and collect your father's gifts.

**2.** Leave the house and try to leave the town to the north. Continue the opening scene until you receive Pikachu.

**3.** Before leaving, talk to the available characters and check the laboratory.

**4.** Go up Route 1 to Viridian City. In the store you will receive the Oak's Parcel during the first visit.

**5.** Return to Oak's lab to deliver the package and receive the Pokédex.

**6.** Head back to Viridian City. From here you can buy Poké Balls and start capturing.

## Recommended preparation (editorial)

- Use Mankey or Bulbasaur as your primary response to Rock-type Pokémon associated with Brock.

- Don't rely exclusively on Pikachu: its electric attacks are not a good response against Rock/Ground combinations.

- If you apply a level cap, use Onix level 12 as a reference. It is a value inherited from Pokémon Yellow, not a cap published by the Pokémon Classic documentation.

- Save before Brock and take several Potions.

## Recommended route

**1.** Completely explore floor 1 before going down. Collect TM09, Paralyze Heal, Potion, Rare Candy, Escape Rope and Moonstone.

**2.** In B1F check the rocks: the source records three Tiny Mushroom and three Big Mushroom.

**4.** Look for the hidden Ether and hit/check the rock containing another Moonstone.

**5.** Before leaving the cave you will have to choose between Fossil Helix and Fossil Dome. The other one is not obtained at this time.

**6.** Prepare for the double battle against Jessie and James before reaching the exit.
