# Cerulean City

<img src="../../images/volumen-02/media/image1.png" style="max-width:100%;height:auto" />

*Town Map from Cerulean City taken from the original guide.*

| **🎯 OBJECTIVE** Resolve the events in the city, prepare for Misty and open the way north. |
|----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## Recommended route

1. Recover your team at the Pokémon Center and check the store.

2. Look for the Rare Candy hidden in the berry yard, next to the fence.

3. Visit the gym when your team is around levels 18-21; You can also complete routes 24 and 25 first to gain experience.

4. After advancing north, return to the house guarded by the agent and defeat the Rocket in the yard to get TM28 Dig.

5. When you are very friendly with the first Pokémon on the team, talk to the breeder in the yard to receive Bulbasaur.

## ⭐ Pokémon and recommended catches

| **Pokémon / group** | **Availability** | **Value for adventure** |
|---------------------|----------------------------------|-----------------------------------------------------|
| Bulbasaur (gift) | Berry yard, with high friendship | Excellent Plant/Poison option for the rest of the game |
| Dratini / Dragonair | Advanced fishing, later | Very rare; does not deserve to delay history now |
| Goldeen / Poliwag | Surf or later fishing | Optional; are not necessary for Misty |

# 🏆 GYM: Misty

<img src="../../images/volumen-02/media/image2.png" style="max-width:100%;height:auto" />

*Interior of Cerulean City's Gym.*

| **RECOMMENDED LEVEL CAP** 21. The highest-level Pokémon on the Pokémon Classic v1.5 team is used as the reference. |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

| **Pokémon** | **Level** | **Type** | **Main hazard** |
|-------------|------------|---------------|-------------------------------------|
| Staryu | 18 | Water | Speed ​​and Water attacks |
| Starmie | 21 | Water/Psychic | Very fast and with high statistics |

## Gym trainers

| **Trainer** | **Team** |
|---|---|
| Picnicker Diana | Goldeen Lv. 19 |
| Swimmer Luis | Horsea Lv. 16 · Shellder Lv. 16 |

## Combat plan

- Pikachu is the natural response, but avoid going several levels below Starmie.

- Bellsprout or Oddish from the northern routes resist Water and can use status changes.

- Don't depend on a single Pokémon: Starmie can outspeed much of the team.

- Bring Potions and heal before starting combat.

| **REWARD** Cascade Badge and TM03 from Pokémon Classic. |
|----------------------------------------------------------|

# Route 24 · Nugget Bridge
<img src="../../images/volumen-02/media/image3.png" style="max-width:100%;height:auto" />

*Route 24 and Nugget Bridge.*

| **🎯 OBJECTIVE** Overcome the chain of trainers, defeat the Rocket and expand the team. |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## Recommended route

6. Cross the bridge by defeating the five trainers in a row.

7. Defeat the Team Rocket disguised recruiter at the end of the bridge.

8. Explore the Grass: Abra is an excellent catch, although you can escape using Teleport.

9. Pick up the TM45 and talk to the characters in the area.

10. Defeat Jessie and James and the extra Rocket before continuing to Route 25.

11. Pick up the static Charmander when it becomes available.
