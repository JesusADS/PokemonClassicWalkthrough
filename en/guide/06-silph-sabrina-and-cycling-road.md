# Route 16

<img src="../../images/volumen-06/media/image1.png" style="max-width:100%;height:auto" />

*Town Map of Route 16.*

| **🎯 OBJECTIVE** Obtain Fly, wake up the second Snorlax and access Cycling Road. |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## Recommended route

1. Enter from Celadon City and use the Poké Flute in front of the second Snorlax. Save before if you want to capture it.

2. Cross the cuttable tree and enter the isolated house to receive the Fly HM.

3. Talk to the berry NPC south of the house daily.

4. After the fifth Badge, visit Cedar on the second floor of the post to receive the Amulet Coin.

5. Defeat the trainers and enter Route 17 with a bike.

## ⭐ Pokémon and recommended catches

|---------------------|------|-----------------------------------------------|

| Muk | 1-4% at night | Rare and resistant |

7. DexNav does not work on this route, so captures depend on normal encounters.

8. Stop in the margins to look for hidden Restore All, Max PP, Rare Candy, Max Revive, and Elixir.

# Saffron City and Dojo Karate

<img src="../../images/volumen-06/media/image4.png" style="max-width:100%;height:auto" />

*Town Map from Saffron City.*

| **🎯 OBJECTIVE** Explore the city, overcome the dojo and prepare for the assault on Silph Co. |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## Recommended route

14. Enter Saffron City through any of the guard posts.

15. Visit Mr. Psychic's house and record his location for the post-game Alakazamite.

16. Buy Agua Fresca and enter the Karate Dojo.

17. Defeat their trainers; water allows you to restore your equipment to repeat combats as EV training.

18. After proving your worth, receive Hitmonlee and Hitmonchan.

19. Next enter Silph Co., occupied by Team Rocket.

## ⭐ Pokémon and recommended catches

| **Pokémon / group** | **Availability** | **Value for adventure** |
|---------------------|------|----------------------------------------------|
| Hitmonlee | Dojo Gift | Fast physical attacker |
| Hitmonchan | Dojo Gift | More versatile; the game allows you to obtain both |

## 💎 Items and rewards

| **Item** | **Where / how** | **Priority** |
|-------------------------|---------------------------------------------|---------------|
| Hitmonlee and Hitmonchan | Dojo Reward | High |
| Alakazamite | House of Mr. Psychic, after the second Champion | Postgame |

| **DOJO AND EV** The guide indicates that the Dojo can be used to train EVs; Bring Agua Fresca to restore your trainers. |
|-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|

## ⚠ Before continuing

- [ ] Complete the Dojo

## Recommended route

20. Explore the lower floors first to collect items and experience.

21. On the fifth floor collect the Magnetic Key; opens the electronic doors of the entire building.

22. Visit the second floor for Thunder Wave's tutor.

23. On the seventh floor, defeat the rival and receive Lapras from the employee.

24. Use the teleportation panels to reach the upper floors.

25. On the eleventh floor defeat Jessie and James and then Giovanni.

26. Talk to the president to receive the Master Ball.

# 🏆 GYM: Sabrina

<img src="../../images/volumen-06/media/image16.png" style="max-width:100%;height:auto" />

*Interior of Sabrina's Gym.*

|-------------|-----------|----------|--------------------------------------------------|
| Abra | 50 | Psychic | Flash and Teleport; does not attack directly |
| Kadabra | 50 | Psychic | Psychic, Recovery and Kinetic |
| Alakazam | 50 | Psychic | Very fast; Psychic, Reflection and Recovery |

## Gym trainers

| **Trainer** | **Team** |
|---|---|
| Psychic Johan | Kadabra Lv. 31 · Slowpoke Lv. 31 · Mr. Mime Lv. 31 · Kadabra Lv. 31 |
| Psychic Tyron | Mr. Mime Lv. 34 · Kadabra Lv. 34 |
| Psychic Cameron | Slowpoke Lv. 33 · Slowpoke Lv. 33 · Slowbro Lv. 33 |
| Psychic Preston | Slowbro Lv. 38 |
| Channeler Amanda | Gastly Lv. 34 · Haunter Lv. 34 |
| Channeler Stacy | Haunter Lv. 38 |
| Channeler Tasha | Gastly Lv. 33 · Gastly Lv. 33 · Haunter Lv. 33 |

## Combat plan

- The three Pokémon have low Physical Defense: use strong physical attacks and neutral type.

- Abra is an opportunity to prepare stat boosts or heal, since there is no direct attack in Pokémon Yellow.

- Kadabra and Alakazam are very fast and hit hard on the special side.

- Avoid Fighting or Poison type Pokémon; They take super effective damage from Psychic.

- Paralysis, sleep, or a fast attacker help control Alakazam before he uses Reflect and Recovery.
