# Mega Evolution

| **REQUIREMENT** |
|--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Defeat Elite Four and the Champion for the second time and get the Mega Ring. Mega stones appear after this milestone. |

- The Pokémon must have its mega stone equipped as a combat item.

- When choosing a move a gray Mega symbol appears. Press START to turn it into a rainbow symbol and confirm the attack.

- The transformation lasts until the Pokémon is weakened or the battle ends.

- Only one Mega Evolution is allowed per combat or encounter.

## Step-by-step walkthrough

1. Start at Viridian Forest and collect Beedrillite.

2. Exit to Route 2 and look for Pidgeotite.

3. Fly to Cerulean City and examine the Berry Backyard for Venusaurite, which is invisible.

4. Go to Mt. Moon and reach 6F for Blastoisinite.

5. Enter Rock Tunnel and drop down to B1F for Aerodactylite.

6. Fly to Lavender Town and climb to 6F from Pokémon Tower for Gengarite.

# Mega Stone Route: Saffron City and Cerulean Cave

| **🎯 OBJECTIVE** |
|---------------------------------------|
| Get Alakazita and the two Mewtwonites. |

<img src="../../images/volumen-12/media/image7.png" style="max-width:100%;height:auto" />

*Cerulean Cave 2F, where both Mewtwonites appear.*

## Step-by-step walkthrough

13. In Saffron City visit Mr. Psychic's house to receive Alakazite.

14. Fly to Cerulean City and enter Cerulean Cave again.

15. Go to the second floor.

16. Pick up Mewtwonite X and Mewtwonite Y, which are now available.

17. Equip the corresponding stone to Mewtwo depending on the form you want to use.
