# How to approach the postgame

| **RECOMMENDED ORDER** |
|---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| 1\) Visit Cedar and check the new followers. 2) Capture Zapdos. 3) Explore Cerulean Cave and capture Mewtwo. 4) Use the Old Sea Map to reach Faraway Island and capture Mew. 5) Complete a second round of the League to unlock the Mega Ring and Mega Stones. |

- The original guide confirms that the game opens rematches, battles for Battle Points, Cerulean Cave, Faraway Island, additional followers and Mega Evolution in the postgame.

- Save before each legendary. Take Ultra Balls, sleep or paralysis moves and a Pokémon that can reduce HP without weakening.

- The source does not provide teams or exact levels of the second League. Therefore, this guide does not invent these data: enter with a team clearly stronger than the one used in the first victory.

## Checklist

**☐** Visit Oak's lab and talk to Cedar

**☐** Check PC and capture objects

**☐** Prepare a capture team

**☐** Buy Ultra Balls and cures

# Cedar and postgame items

| **🎯 OBJECTIVE** |
|------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Collect the capture aids and complete the pending orders from Oak's assistant. |

## Step-by-step walkthrough

1. Return to Pallet Town and enter Oak's laboratory. Talk to the helpers to get clues about Cedar's current position.

2. The guide lists Catching Charm, Oval Charm, and Shiny Charm as Cedar's post-game rewards.

3. Visit its locations in the order indicated by the laboratory; The guide does not publish a complete fixed sequence, so you must follow the clues within the game.

4. Also check your father's gifts on the PC in your room if you left any pending.
5. After becoming Champion, other Pokémon can now be enabled as followers, not just Pikachu.

## 💎 Important objects

| **Item** | **Location** | **Priority** |
|----------------|-----------------------------------|---------------------------------|
| Catching Charm | Cedar's Postgame Reward | Very high |
| Oval Charm | Cedar's Postgame Reward | Average; useful for parenting |
| Shiny Charm | Cedar's Postgame Reward | Very high for shiny search |

| **WHAT THE SOURCE SAYS** |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| If you don't know where Cedar is, talk to the other assistants in Oak's lab. The guide does not list all the intermediate steps or the exact requirements for each charm. |

## Checklist

**☐** Get all three charms

**☐** Try a new Pokémon as a follower

**☐** Check Cedar Clues in the Lab

## Step-by-step walkthrough

6. Fly to the Rock Tunnel entrance on Route 10 and use Surf to reach Power Plant.

7. Go around the building collecting the objects; two Poké Balls are actually Electrode.

8. Get TM25, TM17, Thunder Stones and hidden items before reaching the bottom.

9. Save the game next to the final generator.

10. Face Zapdos. Earth nullifies his electric attacks, but avoid weakening him with too strong hits.

## Step-by-step walkthrough

12. Go north of Cerulean City and cross the Nugget Bridge.

13. Route 24 contains extended story battles against Rocket, Jessie, and James; If they were pending, complete them.

14. Capture the static Charmander if you haven't already.

16. In the postgame a Rocket Fugitive may appear near the cave; Also check the grass on Route 4 if you are doing the daily quest.

17. Enter with Strength, Surf, Repellents, Ultra Balls and many cures.

## Step-by-step walkthrough

18. Completely explore the first floor before taking the stairs; the cave has paths connected by Surf.

19. Collect the Nugget, Restore All, and Max Elixir.

20. On 2F, collect PP Max and the Ultra Ball. The Mewtwonites do not appear until you defeat Oak at the end of the second League.

21. Go down to basement B1F and pick up Revive Max. and Ultra Ball.

22. Save before the final encounter.

23. Mewtwo is the main objective. Use statuses and Psychic-resistant equipment; Avoid poisoning or burning him if the fight could drag on too long.

24. After capturing it, use Escape Rope or return through the exit route.

## Step-by-step walkthrough

25. Return to Pokémon Mansion at Cinnabar Island.

26. In B1F examine the wall indicated in the guide to obtain the Old Sea Map.
27. With the map in your possession, enter the regular house on Cinnabar Island and talk to the old captain. He offers the trip to Faraway Island; the S.S. Anne sailor in Vermilion City does not.

28. Walk along the pier and up to the wooded area.

29. In the second area, follow Mew through the grass until you can start the encounter.

30. Save before interacting and capture Mew with states and Ultra Balls.

31. The guide also records a later event with Lass Kairi when the Faraway Island encounter is captured and you have already completed the first championship.

# Second Pokémon League

| **🎯 OBJECTIVE** |
|-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| Defeat the Elite Four, the rival, and finally Oak; then collect the Mega Ring from Bill at your house. |

## Step-by-step walkthrough

32. Train the party to around level 65 and improve moves, held items, and IVs/EVs if you wish.

33. Buy enough Full Restores, Max Revives, and Elixirs.

34. Defeat Lorelei, Bruno, Agatha, and Lance again. Their postgame teams range from levels 65 to 70.

35. In the Challenger Room, fight the rival first. Five Pokémon are level 65 and the Eevee evolution is level 70.

36. The final battle of this second League run is against Oak, not the rival. His team is Ditto Lv. 69, Tauros Lv. 69, Chansey Lv. 69, Arcanine Lv. 69, Exeggutor Lv. 69, and Rhydon Lv. 70.

37. After defeating Oak and recording the second victory, return to your house in Pallet Town and talk to Bill, who will be waiting there, to receive the Mega Ring.

38. From that point onward, Mega Stones appear as visible golden rocks and new special Trainers are available throughout Kanto.
