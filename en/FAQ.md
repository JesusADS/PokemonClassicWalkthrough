# Frequently asked questions

## Where is Cedar?

Cedar travels through different checkpoints and Pokémon Centers as you collect Badges. If you don't know where it is, talk to the other assistants in Oak's lab for a clue.

Known items and requirements are gathered in the [Cedar item index](REFERENCE-INDEXES.md#items-delivered-by-cedar).

## How do Pokémon that previously required trading evolve?

Kadabra, Machoke, Graveler, and Haunter can evolve with a Power Stone. Other cases involving a held item retain their trade method in the code. See [Special evolutions](SPECIAL-EVOLUTIONS.md) for the full distinction.

## How do I check IVs and EVs?

Open the Pokémon summary and enter the statistics view. The interface displays a prompt to press A to switch between IV and EV views.

The DexNav also represents the quality of IVs by rating them from 0 to 3 stars.

## Why can only Pikachu follow me?

During the main story the behavior of *Pokémon Yellow* is preserved: Pikachu is the available companion. After becoming a Champion, other followers are enabled.

## How do HMs work out of combat?

You can use an HM when you own the corresponding item and have the required Badge. Under the conditions documented by the hack, it does not need to be taught to a Pokémon.

Dig can be used when you own its TM. Teleport requires a compatible Psychic-type Pokémon in your party.

## Why does the Battle Tower function as a store?

The original Battle Tower battle system does not work correctly in this project base. In *Pokémon Classic*, the facility is mainly repurposed as a shop where Battle Points earned from rematches and special battles can be spent.

## I cannot fly to Fuchsia City or the Rock Tunnel entrance

First visit Fuchsia City and the Rock Tunnel entrance on Route 10 on foot. The documentation explains that these visits activate Fly's destinations when its indicators have not yet been registered.

## Where do I get the rods?

| Item | Location |
|---|---|
| Old Rod | Fishing Guru's house in Vermilion City |
| Good Rod | Fishing Guru's house in Fuchsia City |
| Super Rod | Fishing Guru's younger brother on Route 12 |

Encounters that require a rod are marked `🎣` in each volume.

## Does the original documentation list every battle team?

No. The second League teams, certain rematches and various post-game battles are not detailed. The guide identifies them as pending rather than inventing levels, moves or equipped items.

## Can I reuse a save file from an earlier version?

The documentation contains instructions for older migrations between v1.3 and v1.4, but this guide targets v1.5. Check the [current game repository](https://github.com/DaniRainbow/pokeclassic) before moving a save file between versions.
