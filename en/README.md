# Pokémon Classic v1.5 — Complete Walkthrough in English

[← Language selection](../README.md) · [Español](../es/README.md)

Community-made, chronological walkthrough for **Pokémon Classic v1.5**, organized around the Gym challenge, the Pokémon League, and the postgame.

It includes maps, routes, items, recommended catches, trainer tables, Gym Leader teams, Legendary Pokémon, repeatable activities, Mega Evolution, and progress checklists.

## How to use the walkthrough

Every volume follows the natural order of the adventure and contains:

- a recommended step-by-step route;
- original maps of routes, towns, caves, and buildings;
- available Pokémon and recommended catches;
- visible and hidden items, plus content that requires a later return;
- trainer tables and important battles;
- Gym Leader teams based on *Pokémon Yellow* when the hack does not document changes;
- a checklist before leaving each area.

Read [How to use the guide](HOW-TO-USE-THE-GUIDE.md) for the shared symbols, data criteria, structure, and essential mechanics.
